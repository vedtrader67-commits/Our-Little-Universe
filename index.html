Architecture

Firebase Auth handles sign-in.
Firestore stores private trading memory, video analysis records, setup cards and journal entries.
Render backend verifies Firebase ID tokens before calling Gemini or Twelve Data.
Gemini handles video understanding, strategy extraction, Alix chat and screenshot interpretation.
Twelve Data supplies market OHLC data for automatic analysis.
