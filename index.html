Help & FAQ

How does matching work?

Press Start and you're placed in a queue. As soon as another waiting person is available, you're connected automatically.

How do I meet someone new?

Press "Next", or swipe left/right on the video if you're on a touchscreen.

My camera or mic isn't working

Check your browser's site permissions for camera/microphone access, then reload and press Start again.

Someone made me uncomfortable

Use "Report this user" and/or "Block this user" — reported sessions are reviewed, and blocked users can never be matched with you again.

About RandoMeet

RandoMeet connects you with a random person for a live video conversation. build 2026.09 No topics, no scripts — just say hello and see where it goes. Be kind, and remember there's a real person on the other side of the call.

Terms & Safety

You must be 18 or older

RandoMeet is for adults only. Accounts found to belong to minors will be removed.

Be respectful

No nudity, harassment, hate speech, or illegal content. Violations can lead to reports and account removal.

You're responsible for your own safety

Never share personal information (address, financial details, passwords) with strangers you meet here.
